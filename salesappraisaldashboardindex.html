Shared Sales Data Sheet (all departments)

One Google Sheet with every department's manager/month figures — Month, Department, Name, Target, Achieved, and so on, one row per manager per month per department. Paste its link (or just its ID) here; the dashboard reads it once and filters it by department automatically. This never shares, unshares, or changes permissions on any Sheet — each signed-in user still reads it with their own Google account, so they need their own access to it (or "Anyone with the link" needs to stay on) for it to load here.

Manager KRA Master Sheet (optional, per department)

For Leadership/Reporting scores only. Each department can point at its own Master Sheet, or leave this blank if it doesn't have one yet — those two KRAs just show N/A until it's connected.
