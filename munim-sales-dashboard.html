Shared Sales Data Sheet (all departments)

One Google Sheet with every department's manager/month figures — Month, Department, Name, Target, Achieved, and so on, one row per manager per month per department. Paste its link (or just its ID) here; the dashboard reads it once and filters it by department automatically. This never shares, unshares, or changes permissions on any Sheet — each signed-in user still reads it with their own Google account, so they need their own access to it (or "Anyone with the link" needs to stay on) for it to load here.

Quality Data Sheet (for the Quality department)

A separate sheet of QA evaluations — Month, Agent Name, Department, Manager, TL, Feedback Given, Fatal, Communication Score, Compliance Score, Overall Score — one row per evaluation, covering every department. Paste its link (or ID) here; it powers the "Quality" department's own filters and scorecards instead of the regular Target/Achieved cards above.

Manager KRA Master Sheet (optional, per department)

For Leadership/Reporting scores only. Each department can point at its own Master Sheet, or leave this blank if it doesn't have one yet — those two KRAs just show N/A until it's connected.

🟢 Who's online

Everyone who has signed in to this dashboard, and how recently they were last active — refreshes automatically every 20 seconds. This is presence only, tracked entirely inside the dashboard; nothing here comes from Gmail or Google Cloud Console. "Online" means a heartbeat landed in the last ~55 seconds; "Away" means their tab is likely still open but idle or backgrounded; "Offline" means no heartbeat in over 5 minutes (they likely closed the tab).

📝 Edit log

Every figure or KRA score edited from this dashboard, most recent first — who edited it, which manager/month it was on, and the old vs. new value. Only edits made after this feature shipped are captured here; nothing retroactive. Session-only edits are session-only until someone actually changes a value — that's the moment this log records.
